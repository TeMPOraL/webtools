What to look for

• Distant things shrink exponentially fast — the barn almost vanishes from across the meadow.
• Strafe sideways past a tree: perspective twists in a very non-Euclidean way.
• Walk a loop around the pond and you return rotated (holonomy) — direction is path-dependent here.
• The ground rings are 10 m apart; watch how much circumference each successive ring gains.
• The marker posts along the shuttle path are exactly 5 m apart — watch the spacing collapse.
• Sight along the stone wall in the east meadow: it is a perfectly straight geodesic, yet from the side it seems to bend away.
• The windmill's top squeezes toward the eye-level horizon as you leave — on a small planet it would sink bottom-first instead. This world is not a sphere; it is the opposite.
• Drag the curvature slider: small radius = strongly curved, 40 m ≈ nearly flat.
